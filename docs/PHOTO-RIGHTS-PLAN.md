## How a route was judged confirmed

A route is confirmed only if, on 2026-11-24, the page answered HTTP 200 and the address, form or phone number appeared in that page's own HTML, or a prior worker verified it and I re-checked it here. A domain that resolves but serves a parking page, a parked lander, or nothing at all is **not** a route, and the brand is marked `route unconfirmed` rather than given a guessed address.

## Before anything is sent

1. Tanvi approves this exact wording, brand by brand. Nothing leaves the mailbox without that.
2. Each send is recorded against the brand with the date, the address used and the message id.
3. A written yes is recorded with `node tools/photo-permissions.mjs --grant "<Brand>" --ref "Email from <name>, <date>"`, which publishes that brand's staged photographs and writes the licence reference into the register.
4. A no, or no answer after two follow-ups, leaves the illustrations in place. The illustration is the safe default, not a failure.

## What this plan does not do

- It does not send anything.
- It does not publish or import a single photograph.
- It does not restore any Dentalkart-sourced image, and no Dentalkart image is cited as a route.
- It does not edit `src/data/image-rights.ts`.

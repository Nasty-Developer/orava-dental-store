# Image permission request: draft for approval

Nothing has been sent. Tanvi must approve this wording before any email leaves
the connected Gmail account.

## Who it goes to

Manufacturer marketing or India distributor contact, one email per brand, sent
from Tanvi's address. Start with the brands already in the catalogue that matter
commercially, not all 130 at once.

## Subject

Permission to use official product images on our dental supplies website

## Body

Dear [Brand] team,

I run SOMIL Dental Store (somil-dental-products.vercel.app), an Indian dental
supplies storefront that sells [Brand] products to dental practices.

I would like permission to display your official product photographs on our
product pages, and to receive your standard image pack if one exists.

Specifically, I am asking whether we may:

1. Host your official product images on our own servers and CDN.
2. Display them alongside the product name, specification and pack size.
3. Keep them for as long as the product is listed with us.

We will not alter the images beyond resizing and format conversion for web, will
not crop out brand identification, safety marks or regulatory labels, and will
credit [Brand] as the manufacturer on every page. We do not use images from other
retailers.

If you have a preferred image pack or brand asset guidelines, please send them and
we will follow them exactly. If permission needs to come from your Indian
distributor, I would be grateful if you could point me to the right contact.

Happy to confirm anything you need in writing.

Kind regards,
Tanvi Tapase
SOMIL Dental Store
+91 85914 34914

## What a reply has to contain to change anything

A written statement that we may use the images on our website. A verbal yes, a
generic marketing brochure, or a distributor saying "sure" without authority does
not move a row from `none` to `granted` in the audit.

## Tracking

Every reply gets recorded against the affected products in the audit CSV, with the
date, the sender and the exact permission granted. Until then the rows stay as they
are and nothing new publishes.

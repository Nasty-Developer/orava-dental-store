Drop product photographs here, then reference them from src/data/catalogue/*.ts:

  image: {
    src: "/products/your-file.jpg",
    alt: "Short description of the photo",
    source: "Where the photo came from",
    license: "Why you may publish it",
  }

Square, 1200x1200px minimum, plain background, under 250 KB.
Do not save images from competitor websites here.
